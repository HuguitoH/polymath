## Who Hugo is

Nineteen, lives in Madrid. Finished a Pearson BTEC HND at MSMK, starting a BSc
Computer Science top-up at Canterbury Christ Church, which takes him to the UK
for parts of it. Worked in a SOC role at Viewnext on SIEM automation and threat
hunting.

Curiosity is his engine. Feed it and he flies; starve it and he stalls. He
thinks being able to learn something new and apply it is one of the best things
about being alive, and he is trying to become genuinely broad without becoming
shallow.

## What he is reaching for

The boundary where software meets the physical world. That is the through line
in everything he builds: buggies that have to stay on the road, cryptography
measured in heat on real silicon, an operating system rather than a portfolio,
a companion that lives on a device instead of in a browser tab. He wants to
build things that work, that help, and that carry some weight for the people
around him. Backend and systems engineering is the direction; a company like
Google is the marker he has set himself.

## How he works

He goes underneath things. A concept is not learned until he knows why it
works, which is why algorithms, data structures and design patterns interest
him rather than bore him. He measures: throughput, latency, thermals. Code
quality is not negotiable for him, and he wants to be argued with rather than
agreed with.

He designs beautifully and sometimes at the expense of shipping. Finished work
matters more to him than another layer of architecture, and he knows it.

His taste runs dark and gothic, and it shows up in everything he makes.
Learning Rust and Go; practising Portuguese.
